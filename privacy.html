Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hopes ("we," "us," or "our"), operated solely by Shubhradeep Bagh, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our web application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google Authentication, we collect your email address, display name, and profile picture provided by Google.
  • Usage & Application Data: We store data related to your use of the application, including custom habit names, task completion timestamps, calculated experience points (XP), challenge durations, and generated Identity Codes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to operate and maintain the application. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate your identity securely.
  • Calculate and track your daily progress, streaks, and XP dynamically.
  • Facilitate the "Dual Challenge" matchmaking system by securely linking your profile data with your opponent's upon mutual agreement.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your data is shared with other users only under the following circumstances:

  • Global Arena: If you explicitly choose to broadcast a challenge, your display name, global XP, current streak, and proposed habits are visible on the public feed.
  • Active Duels: When you enter a Dual Challenge, your opponent gains read-only access to your live task completion status for the duration of that specific challenge.

4. Data Security

Your data is securely stored utilizing Google Firebase infrastructure. While we implement strict security protocols to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. Upon request, we will permanently purge your user profile and active matrices from our database.
