Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

To operate the Hopes accountability matrix, we collect the following information:

  • Authentication Data: Your Google Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture via Google Auth.
  • Matrix Data: Your custom habits, task names, completion timestamps, daily XP, and matrix duration settings.
  • Community Data: Your generated 6-digit Identity Code and the unique IDs of users you engage with in Dual Challenges.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase. We use it strictly to calculate your XP, maintain your streaks, facilitate the midnight rollover logic, and sync your live progress with your active Dual Challenge partners. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Visibility

Your Personal Silence matrix is completely private. If you engage in a Dual Challenge, your opponent will only be able to view your overall completion percentage, your live XP, and the specific tasks you configured for that shared duel.
